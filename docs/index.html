Architecture

Four stages. One coherent system.

Stage 01 Adapter Training Three condition-specific LoRA adapters, reverb, noise, codec, trained independently on augmented LibriSpeech mixtures.
Stage 02 Gate Training Level-1 DSP features (SNR, codec bandwidth, voiced density) + Level-2 learned E(0) heads drive a soft gating network.
Stage 03 Joint Fine-tune End-to-end PIT SI-SDR optimization of gate + LoRA mixture. 14 epochs, temperature-calibrated (T=4.987).
Stage 04 Band Recovery Oracle-trained BandRecoveryHead: 8→16 kHz in 58 s of training. Best loss 0.012, bypasses SR-CorrNet bottleneck.
